Reading a Canyon Wall Like a Sentence

Stand at the base of any layered canyon and you are looking at a paragraph written in stone. The thick pale bands mark long, calm stretches of deposition. The thin dark lines mark sudden events — a flood, an ash fall, a shift in the wind.
